Privacy policy

Note contents, photos and protection settings are stored locally on your device. Update checks do not transmit them to a server.

When viewing this policy or checking for new versions, the app connects to bluetoothdisable.app over HTTPS. Update checks run in the main interface: automatically at most once a day or when requested by the user. Public update checks are disabled in the internal build.

These connections expose standard network information to the website and its Cloudflare infrastructure, including your IP address, request time, requested address and technical HTTP headers. The update client uses a generic User-Agent without the installed version number and sends no cookies, tokens, device identifiers or protection data. No analytics has been added to the update mechanism.

The Details button opens the GitHub release page in an external browser application. GitHub and your browser’s privacy practices apply after that navigation.
